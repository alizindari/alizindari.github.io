## Current Cover Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Generate a NEW wide 3:1 blog cover for a candid, slightly darkly funny personal post called 'The Iced Tea No One Wanted'. It must feel like a quick, observant sketch in a researcher's notebook, NOT a kitchen advertisement or a polished still life. A spare loose black-ink doodle on clean white paper with just a few flat muted teal and amber brush marks: an ordinary unopened plastic iced-tea bottle standing alone on a single roughly drawn counter line, next to a small crooked handwritten paper note reading exactly 'FREE'. Off to one side, an empty disposable paper plate with three cake crumbs, drawn with just a few casual strokes. Bottle, note, and plate form one simple, legible composition centered within the wide frame, with generous white margins around them and nothing cut off. Imperfect human pen lines, uneven hatching, understated dry humor, economical drawing like an independent magazine spot illustration. Make the unopened full bottle immediately recognizable but unbranded, with a tiny plain label and a cap; no luxurious packaging. No faces or anthropomorphic expressions. No kitchen interior, no tiles, no plants, no mugs, no utensils, no window, no cabinets, no pretty sunlight, no glassy product-rendering, no realistic materials, no beige watercolor wash, no motivational text, no arrows, no charts, no decorative circles. Absolutely no text except the one word FREE on the small note. Do not draw the blog title. Output a finished panoramic 3:1 bitmap illustration suitable for a blog thumbnail.

## Generation Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished editorial blog cover image for a personal essay titled 'The Iced Tea No One Wanted'. This is a project image, not a website mockup. Wide panoramic composition, aspect ratio exactly 3:1, ideally 2400 x 800 pixels. Scene: the shared kitchen counter in a university research building. One unopened, full, modest clear bottle of amber iced tea with a green screw cap and a very simple unbranded pale label is the central focal point. To its left, a used white cake plate with just a few crumbs is empty. Beside the bottle, a small propped-up paper note says exactly 'Please help yourself' in natural handwritten lettering. The bottle is clearly still sealed and untouched. These ordinary objects gently imply that the cake was taken but the free tea was ignored. No people. No faces on objects. No trash can or dramatic symbols. Composition is spare, observant, intimate and slightly wry, not bleak or tragic. The whole bottle including cap, whole note, and plate must fit comfortably within the frame with safe margins; no cropped main objects. Background: a quiet, understated off-white and muted blue-green tiled kitchen wall, lightly suggested cabinetry, pale cool-gray counter. Style: sophisticated hand-painted editorial illustration with fine ink outlines, delicate gouache or watercolor texture, restrained detail, clean recognizable silhouettes, soft natural daylight, subtle shadows, sage/teal accents and amber tea, mostly light background. NOT glossy 3D, NOT a vector infographic, NOT a corporate stock illustration. No title, no branding, no equations, no decorative badges, no extra text beyond the small note, no border or frame. The image should read immediately when displayed as a 3:1 blog thumbnail.
